A few books I've genuinely enjoyed, roughly split between systems work and
everything else. Each title links to its page on Open Library. None of these are
affiliate links.

## Systems and engineering

- [*Designing Data-Intensive Applications*](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19293745W) — Martin Kleppmann
- [*Designing Distributed Systems: Patterns and Paradigms for Scalable, Reliable Systems Using Kubernetes*](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL19543542W) — Brendan Burns
- [*Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship*](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17618370W) — Robert C. Martin

## Memoir and fiction

- [*Skunk Works: A Personal Memoir of My Years at Lockheed*](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL3466236W) — Ben R. Rich
- [*The Count of Monte Cristo*](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL20201553W) — Alexandre Dumas (public domain, free to read)
